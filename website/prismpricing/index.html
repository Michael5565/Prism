Questions, answered.

How does activation work?

After checkout, Prism shows your license key right here. Copy it into the Prism extension pane to unlock Pro.

Is there a free trial?

Prism includes a seven-day trial in the extension, with no card required. Pick a plan when you are ready to keep Pro.

Is my Drive data private?

Yes. Prism works in your browser and does not send your document contents to our servers. Read the privacy policy.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from the link in your Paddle receipt.
