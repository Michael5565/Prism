Prism — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026 · Contact: support@getwalksafe.co.uk

Prism runs entirely in your browser. It searches inside the Google Drive files you can already see on drive.google.com / docs.google.com and shows passages matching your Drive search query. We do not collect, store, or sell your document contents.

Data we handle

1. Document text (ephemeral)

When you search Drive, Prism discovers file IDs visible on the page, then fetches each file’s text via your own Google session (credentials: include — export?format=txt and drive.usercontent.google.com/download). Text lives only in chrome.storage.local under doc_{fileId} for 1 hour, then is evicted or on quota pressure. It is never sent to our servers.

2. Search count & license

prismSearchCount, prismPremium, prismLicenseKey, themePreference — stored locally. When you enter a license key, Prism POSTs {key} to https://prism-license-worker.purrapi.workers.dev/api/validate-license (Cloudflare Worker + KV). We store only key status and expiry.

3. What we do NOT do

  • No Google OAuth scopes. No drive.readonly. We only harvest IDs you already see in the DOM.
  • No analytics, ads, or fingerprinting. No remote code.

Retention & deletion

Uninstalling removes all local data. KV records expire after 1 year or when Paddle cancels. Email us to delete a license record.
