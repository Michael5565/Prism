Prism runs in your browser. It searches files you can already see in Google Drive and helps you change the appearance of Google Docs and Sheets. We do not collect, store, or sell your document contents.

Data we handle

Document text — temporarily

When you search Drive, Prism finds visible file IDs and fetches matching text using your own Google session. Text is kept only in chrome.storage.local for up to one hour to make repeat searches faster. It is never sent to Prism servers.

Search count and license

prismSearchCount, prismPremium, prismLicenseKey, and theme preferences are stored locally. If you enter a license key, Prism sends only that key to prism-license-worker.purrapi.workers.dev to confirm its status and expiry.

What we do not do

No Google OAuth scopes. No analytics, ads, fingerprinting, or remote code. Prism does not upload your documents.

Permissions

PERMISSIONWHY
storageCache document text, remember themes and license state.
docs.google.comSupport Docs, Sheets, and visible file exports.
drive.google.comFind visible file IDs and downloads in your Drive.
prism-license-worker.purrapi.workers.devValidate Prism Pro licenses.

Retention and deletion

Uninstalling Prism removes its local data. License records are retained only for billing and validation, then expire when cancelled or after their service period. Contact us to request deletion of a license record.
