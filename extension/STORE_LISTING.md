## Detailed Description

**Supercharge Google Drive with deep in-document search and an eye-friendly, image-safe Dark Mode for Google Docs.**

Stop wasting time opening doc after doc and pressing Ctrl+F. Prism brings powerful full-text search directly into Google Drive (`drive.google.com` and `docs.google.com`) and gives Google Docs a native-quality dark mode that never inverts your images into negatives.

Whenever you search in Drive, Prism scans your Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, and PDFs, highlighting exact sentence matches and letting you jump straight to the exact paragraph with a single click.

---

### 🔍 Key Features

- **Full-Text Deep Search**: Searches every word inside your documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and PDF files.
- **1-Click Jump to Paragraph**: Click any snippet in the search results to open the Google Doc and jump directly to that exact line or paragraph.
- **Image-Safe Docs Dark Mode**: True dark mode for Google Docs that transforms text and backgrounds without inverting images, charts, diagrams, or photos into photo negatives.
- **Multi-Format Support**: Works seamlessly across Google Docs (.gdoc, .docx), Sheets (.gsheet, .xlsx, .csv), Slides (.gslides, .pptx), and PDFs.
- **Highlighted Context Snippets**: See the exact surrounding sentence and context before opening any document.
- **Instant File-Type Filtering**: Filter results in real-time across Docs, Sheets, Slides, or PDFs with live match counters.
- **Seamless Drive Integration**: Works directly with Google Drive's native search bar without changing your workflow.

---

### 🔒 100% Private & Secure by Design

- **Client-Side Only**: Runs entirely in your local browser using your existing Google session.
- **Zero Data Harvesting**: No OAuth broad permissions required. Your documents and search terms are never uploaded to any external server.
- **Local Cache**: File text is securely cached locally in your browser for 1 hour to ensure lightning-fast repeated searches, then automatically cleared.

---

### 🚀 How to Use

1. Install the extension and open **drive.google.com** or **docs.google.com**.
2. Type any search term into Google Drive's search bar.
3. The Prism search panel slides open, displaying real-time text matches and contextual snippets.
4. Click any snippet to jump straight to the exact sentence or paragraph inside the document!

### 📋 Permissions Explained

- `storage`: Caches scanned document text locally in your browser for 1 hour for fast results and tracks search counts.
- `offscreen`: Enables fast, in-browser PDF text extraction and document parsing.
- Host permissions (`drive.google.com`, `docs.google.com`, `*.googleusercontent.com`): Allows Prism to fetch and read file contents within your active browser session.
- License verification (`*.workers.dev`): Securely activates Pro licenses via paddle/workers.
